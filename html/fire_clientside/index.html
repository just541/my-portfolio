Fire Protection System

What is a fire protection system?

A fire protection system is a comprehensive set of measures, equipment, and procedures designed to detect, control, suppress, and mitigate fires to protect people, property, and the environment. These systems are put in place to minimize the damage caused by fires and to ensure the safety of occupants in buildings and other structures.

In this case, this fire protection system is being developed to serve as an instrument for two key stakeholders: the BFP response team and homeowners. This system aims to help the BFP response team and as well as to the homeowners to transmit real-time notification in which it can help prevent and reduce future incidents.

Rationale

Internet of Things (IoT)

In today's age of growing connectivity and the Internet of Things (IoT), it makes sense to advance smoke detection by integrating smart technology. This enables seamless communication and control with existing home or building automation systems.

Early Detection & Response

While traditional smoke detectors depend on occupants to hear alarms and respond, smart smoke detection systems can identify smoke or fire in unoccupied spaces, leading to faster response times and potential life and property-saving benefits.

Real-Time Notification

In case of a fire, the system can automatically transmit alerts, including location information, to the BFP, ensuring rapid response and minimizing potential harm and damage.

BFP Mandate

The BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION was created by virtue of R.A. 6975 primarily to perform the following functions:

Be responsible for the prevention and suppression of all destructive fires on:

  1. Buildings, houses and other structures;
  2. Forest;
  3. Land transportation vehicles and equipment;
  4. Ships or vessels docked at piers or wharves or anchored in major
  5. Petroleum industry installations;
  6. Plane crashes; and
  7. Other similar incidents

Be responsible for the enforcement of the Fire Code of the Philippines (P.D. 1185) and other related laws.

Shall have the power to investigate all causes of fires and, if necessary, file the proper complaint with the city or provincial prosecutor who has jurisdiction over the case.

In time of national emergency, all elements of the BFP shall, upon direction of the President, assist the AFP in meeting national emergency, and

Shall establish at least one (1) fire station with adequate personnel, firefighting facilities and equipment in every city subject to standards, rules and regulations as may be promulgated by the Department of the Interior and Local Government.
